CleanBridge DFW - Account Deletion

CleanBridge DFW users may request deletion of their account and associated personal data at any time.

How to Request Account Deletion

To request deletion of your CleanBridge DFW account, send an email to:

Email: nikoautocore2@gmail.com

Please use the subject line: CleanBridge DFW Account Deletion Request

In your email, include the email address associated with your CleanBridge DFW account so we can identify the correct account.

What Will Be Deleted

After verifying the request, we will delete the user's CleanBridge DFW account and personal data associated with that account, except information that we are required to retain for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or record-keeping purposes.

Deletion Requests

We will process valid account deletion requests within a reasonable period of time.
