CleanBridge DFW Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 19, 2026

CleanBridge DFW respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use the CleanBridge DFW application, how that information is used, and the choices available to users.

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account or use CleanBridge DFW, we may collect information that you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Service or job address
  • Cleaning or service request information
  • Requested service dates and times
  • Customer quotes
  • Cleaner offers
  • Job status and completion information
  • Payment or payout preference information

2. How We Use Information

We use information to provide and operate CleanBridge DFW, including to:

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Process service requests
  • Connect customers, cleaners, and dispatch personnel
  • Send and manage quotes
  • Track job progress
  • Confirm completed services
  • Coordinate payments and cleaner payouts
  • Provide customer support
  • Protect the security and integrity of the service

3. Payments

CleanBridge DFW does not process credit card or bank account payments directly inside the application. Payments may be completed outside the app using a payment method selected by the parties involved.

Users should not enter credit card numbers, debit card numbers, bank account passwords, PIN numbers, or other sensitive banking credentials into CleanBridge DFW.

4. Account Roles

CleanBridge DFW uses separate account roles for customers, cleaners, and dispatch personnel. Information shown to each account type may be limited to information necessary to provide the requested service and operate the platform.

5. Service Providers

CleanBridge DFW may use third-party service providers for functions such as account authentication, database hosting, application hosting, security, and technical infrastructure.

These providers may process information only as necessary to provide their services and are subject to their own privacy and security practices.

6. Sharing of Personal Information

CleanBridge DFW does not sell personal information.

Information may be shared only when reasonably necessary to:

  • Provide a requested cleaning or related service
  • Connect a customer with an approved cleaner
  • Operate and maintain the application
  • Prevent fraud or misuse
  • Protect users and the service
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements

7. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect personal information. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain legitimate business records, prevent fraud, resolve disputes, and comply with legal obligations.

9. Account and Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of their CleanBridge DFW account and associated personal information by contacting us by email.

10. Children's Privacy

CleanBridge DFW is intended for adults who request or provide cleaning and related services. The service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
